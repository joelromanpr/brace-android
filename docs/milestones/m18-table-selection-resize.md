## Source in this slice

- `BraceTableSelection` represents one controlled cell, row, column, or inclusive rectangular range by stable keys. A cell long press and endpoint tap, Shift plus keyboard navigation, Shift plus mouse click, and a TalkBack custom action extend a range. The focused table announces its selection; visible cells expose selected state.
- `BraceDataTable` accepts controlled column-width and row-height maps, change callbacks, and optional maximums. Token-styled grips have at least 48 dp targets, pointer drag, keyboard increments, and localized TalkBack increase/decrease actions. Size inputs must be finite; controlled values clamp to theme minimums. Row-header width measures the localized corner label and largest row number at the current font scale. Main's bounded Compose width check and explicit row-major traversal hints remain in place.
- The pinned table resizing and cell-selection rows are **in progress** with implementation, catalog, documentation, and test links. The Region row remains planned because disjoint selections and whole-table selection are absent. Of 23 main-track table rows, 7 are in progress and 16 remain planned.
- The [catalog](../../catalog/src/main/java/io/github/joelromanpr/brace/catalog/TableCatalogSample.kt) has a 120-row interactive sample with selection and size controls. The [usage guide](../table-selection-resize.md), Pages guide, and independent Maven consumer exercise the public API. The sample was extracted into its own composable to keep the catalog's JVM method under the bytecode limit.

## Limits and next table slices

Only one contiguous range is supported. Disjoint regions, mouse drag selection, touch auto-scroll while extending a range, whole-table selection, copying, editing, frozen data regions, sorting, and row/column reordering remain planned. Manual TalkBack spoken-order QA remains open; Compose traversal hints and native node checks are automated. The catalog's fixed chrome may crowd the table sample at 3× text on a 320 × 640 phone; use a larger viewport for that appearance review. Later table branches add copying and controlled cell editing.
